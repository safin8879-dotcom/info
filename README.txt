OMAR FARUK SARKAR — RADIOGRAPHER PORTFOLIO
==========================================

Deployment:
1. Extract this ZIP file.
2. Upload index.html to your hosting public_html folder, or upload the extracted folder to Netlify.
3. Open your domain to view the website.

The website is self-contained. The supplied profile and gallery photos are embedded in index.html.
All four previous Experience entries have been preserved, and the three additional entries have been appended.
